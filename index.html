About Me

Background & Education

I am Zhang Yichi, a graduate researcher and developer pursuing a master's degree at China Agricultural University, working at the intersection of animal genetics, breeding, and bioinformatics.

My research focuses on genomic and transcriptomic analyses of livestock. Ongoing projects include breed identification of goats and sheep using machine learning, as well as single-cell differential gene expression analysis to dissect the genetic architecture underlying complex traits. I am passionate about building computational pipelines that bridge raw sequencing data and meaningful biological insights.

Outside the lab, I enjoy exploring open-source tools and building software to make scientific workflows more reproducible and accessible.
